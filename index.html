Process

From sketch to gown

  1. 01 Moodboard

    A painting, a garden, a palette. Concept, colour story, texture and fabric families come first.

  2. 02 Sketch & drawing

    Design development in pencil and watercolour, then technical drawings for every look.

  3. 03 Execution

    Cut, draped and sewn by hand. Each flower is shaped one at a time, each slightly different from the last.
