About

Sahar Raad

Sahar Raad is a Lebanese designer based in Beirut. Trained first as an architect, with a master's in landscape design from the Politecnico di Milano, she came to couture through proportion, structure and a love of place.

In the atelier of Michele and Mariette Atallah she learned the craft from first draft to final fitting. Her own pieces carry that discipline lightly: a painting, a garden, a single feeling, turned into form, detail and story.
